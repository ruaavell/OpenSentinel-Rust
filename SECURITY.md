# OpenSentinel — Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security bug in OpenSentinel, **please do
not open a public issue.** Email a description, the affected version, and
any reproduction steps to:

    security@ruaavell.dev

We will acknowledge receipt within 5 business days and aim for a patch
within 30 days. Please give us a reasonable window to review a report
before you publish details.

## Supported releases

Only the latest released version on the `main` branch is supported.

## Out of scope

- Issues on third-party engines (ClamAV, YARA) — report to their maintainers.
- Social engineering, phishing, or other non-code attack surfaces.

## Disclosure policy

We do not disclose a reporter's identity without permission, and we will
credit you by name if you wish.
